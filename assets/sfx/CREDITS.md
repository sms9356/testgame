# School sound cues

Source: **Bienenkorbglocke.1133.Hz.ogg**, Membeth (2012-12-28), CC0 1.0.
Original recording and license: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bienenkorbglocke.1133.Hz.ogg
Free WAV distribution used: https://github.com/code4fukui/sound-cc0/blob/main/bell1.wav
Distribution ledger: https://github.com/code4fukui/sound-cc0/blob/main/sounds.csv
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

`school-bell.mp3`, `class-chime.mp3`, `pa-chime.mp3` are game adaptations of the recorded bell: trimmed, repitched, sequenced, filtered, given a short reflection, and normalized. These are designed school cues using a real bell sample; they are not field recordings of an actual school's PA system. No recorded speech is included.

Rebuild with `python tools/build_school_sfx.py /path/to/bell1.wav` (numpy + ffmpeg). Source and output SHA-256 values are in manifest.json. CC0 permits commercial use, modification and redistribution. Attribution is retained voluntarily.
